Fit Mark is an offline bicycle setup journal for recreational riders and home mechanics. Keep ride impressions, including terrain, comfort rating, and optional duration and distance, on your device. Export a JSON backup or plain-text setup sheet using your device's file picker. Preview counts before confirming a JSON import that replaces local data. No account or tracking is required.

Current release limits: the Setup screen does not yet let you add bikes or enter your own measurements or dated setup snapshots. Comparison of setup changes is not available in the app yet. Setup sheets are empty until bikes can be entered. Do not rely on Fit Mark for medical advice or a professional bike-fit prescription. Exported files may contain personal notes; protect and store them yourself.
