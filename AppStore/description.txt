Fit Mark is a planned offline bicycle setup journal for recreational riders and home mechanics. Record measured bike contact points with units and reference methods, save dated adjustment snapshots, compare meaningful changes, and keep ride notes. Planned exports let you carry your own setup sheet and backup between devices. Designed for phone, tablet, and Windows desktop with no account, ads, or tracking. Fit Mark is not medical advice or a professional fit prescription. Features described here are planned and not yet shipped.
